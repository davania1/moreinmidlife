Meet the Heart Behind Her Midlife Movement

Hi, I'm Davania.

I've always believed life is meant to be lived fully.

I'm a woman of faith, mother, grandmother, speaker, mentor, traveler, fitness enthusiast, Project Consultant, real estate professional, and curator of meaningful experiences. My faith is the foundation of everything I do, and my family is where I find my greatest joy and grounding.

Her Midlife Movement grew from the life I was already living — encouraging women, building community, staying active, exploring new places, and creating thoughtful experiences that bring people together.

I find joy in the details: the carefully planned itinerary, the handwritten note, the perfect gathering space, the unexpected surprise, and the moments people remember long after an experience ends.

Through Her Midlife Movement, I bring together faith, movement, travel, encouragement, and genuine connection to inspire women to embrace midlife with confidence, joy, and purpose.

My hope is that every woman who encounters this community feels seen, encouraged, and reminded that there is always more waiting for her.

The Heart Behind It All

Why I Do This

I do this because I believe women are worth it.

Not someday. Not when the kids are grown, the mortgage is paid, or the timing is perfect. Now. In this season. At this age. With this body, this faith, and this beautiful, complicated, full life.

I've watched too many women pour endlessly into everyone around them — their families, their careers, their communities — while quietly shelving their own dreams, adventures, and joy. Telling themselves there will be time later. That it's too expensive. That they're too old. That they've missed their window.

I don't believe that. Not for a single second.

Movement matters because our bodies were made to move — not just in fitness, but in life. To explore new places. To step into new rooms. To keep growing, learning, and saying yes to things that scare us a little.

Friendships matter because we were never meant to do life alone. The right community — women who see you, cheer for you, travel with you, pray with you, and laugh until you cry with you — that's not a luxury. That's medicine.

We don't stop living simply because we're in midlife. If anything, midlife is when we finally have the wisdom to live fully. We know what matters. We've survived what we thought would break us. We've learned who we are.

And God placed this vision on my heart because He knows what women need — and He chose me to help create it. That's not something I take lightly. Every experience I curate is an act of obedience, love, and service.

This is why I do this.
And I'm so grateful you found your way here.

Who We Are

More than an experience.
A way of living richly.

Her Midlife Movement is a lifestyle community created for women who are ready to move, travel, connect, and live more fully in every season.

We bring together movement, meaningful travel, faith, friendship, and thoughtfully curated experiences that encourage women to reconnect with themselves, embrace new possibilities, and enjoy the richness of midlife.

This is a space for joy, restoration, adventure, honest conversation, and becoming more of who you were created to be.

What We Stand For

Our Five Foundations

01
Connection

Creating spaces where rich, meaningful relationships are built — and where you experience the power of genuine community with women who truly see you.

02
Movement

Moving your body. Moving into new experiences. Moving beyond fear and stagnation. Moving through life's transitions with courage and grace.

03
Curated Experience

The details matter. Beautiful environments, meaningful conversations, shared meals, reflection, surprise moments, and elevated touches that make you feel truly cared for.

04
Mindset & Faith

Midlife isn't about becoming someone new — it's about becoming more of who you were created to be. We believe faith gives us the courage to keep growing, the strength to keep moving, and the peace to embrace every new season with purpose.

05
Rest + Joy + Adventure

Our experiences blend wellness, travel, friendship, fun, exploration, self-care, and personal growth — because you deserve all of it, all at once.
