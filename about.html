Meet Davania

Davania · Founder
Meet the Heart Behind Her Midlife Movement

Hi, I'm Davania.

I've always believed life is meant to be lived fully.

I'm a woman of faith, mother, grandmother, speaker, mentor, traveler, fitness enthusiast, Project Consultant, real estate professional, and curator of meaningful experiences. My faith is the foundation of everything I do, and my family is where I find my greatest joy and grounding.

Her Midlife Movement grew from the life I was already living — encouraging women, building community, staying active, exploring new places, and creating thoughtful experiences that bring people together.

I find joy in the details: the carefully planned itinerary, the handwritten note, the perfect gathering space, the unexpected surprise, and the moments people remember long after an experience ends.

Through Her Midlife Movement, I bring together faith, movement, travel, encouragement, and genuine connection to inspire women to embrace midlife with confidence, joy, and purpose.

My hope is that every woman who encounters this community feels seen, encouraged, and reminded that there is always more waiting for her.

The Heart Behind It All

Why I Do This

I do this because I believe women are worth it.

Not someday. Not when the kids are grown, the mortgage is paid, or the timing is perfect. Now. In this season. At this age. With this body, this faith, and this beautiful, complicated, full life.

I've watched too many women pour endlessly into everyone around them — their families, their careers, their communities — while quietly shelving their own dreams, adventures, and joy.

I don't believe that. Not for a single second.

Movement matters because our bodies were made to move — not just in fitness, but in life. To explore new places. To step into new rooms. To keep growing, learning, and saying yes to things that scare us a little.

Friendships matter because we were never meant to do life alone. The right community — women who see you, cheer for you, travel with you, pray with you, and laugh until you cry with you — that's not a luxury. That's medicine.

We don't stop living simply because we're in midlife. If anything, midlife is when we finally have the wisdom to live fully.

And God placed this vision on my heart because He knows what women need — and He chose me to help create it.

This is why I do this. And I'm so grateful you found your way here.
